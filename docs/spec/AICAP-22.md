# AICAP-22: Later: define AI budgets together with subscription pricing from quality and cost evidence

Future product decision; not an initial release dependency

Do not impose monetary AI budgets or subscription allowances initially. First establish high-quality music notation transcription, measure actual usage/cost and optimize while preserving quality.

Before defining pricing

- Gather representative per-page/job usage, retries, cost, latency, transcription errors and human review effort.

- Compare quality/cost trade-offs on actual ensemble music pieces; do not pick arbitrary budget amounts first.

- Use the measured evidence to define subscription pricing and AI budget/allowance rules together with the user.

- Determine scope, periods, exhaustion behavior, overages and enforcement only after that product decision. No numerical allowance, billing integration or paid subscription implementation is approved by this ticket.

Dependencies

AICAP-12 high-quality transcription and AICAP-14 usage/cost measurement. This ticket is later work and does not block the initial refactor.
